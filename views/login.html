Scrubs

Count the shifts you actually worked.

Scrubs reads your Google Calendar, matches your shift titles against counters you define once, and turns the roster you already keep into numbers you can use.

Scrubs asks for read-only access to your calendars and the configuration spreadsheet. Nothing is sent anywhere else — the page talks to Google directly from your browser, and there is no server in between.

This sheet holds your counters and your default view. It is set per deployment — change it here only if you keep your configuration somewhere else. The override is remembered in this browser only.

Any date range you like, and your preferred one saved back to the configuration sheet.
